# Chispa v2 acceptance

- Home is generic and does not frame Chispa as a solar app.
- Solar exists under Projects and owns its Wiring/Supplier subviews.
- Need to have and Nice to have filters work.
- Lists and projects can be created and items can be added.
- USD and MXN are derived from one rate, not independent hard-coded totals.
- Offers compare landed cost across currencies.
- Search opens Amazon México, Mercado Libre México, Amazon USA, Temu, and Google Web.
- Local suppliers expose call and Google/Apple/Waze navigation; online stores do not show physical navigation.
- Local data autosaves and v1 state is migrated.
- JSON, CSV, print/PDF, read-only sharing and WhatsApp summaries exist.
- PWA updates do not remain permanently pinned to the v1 cache.
- No production claim of automatic live price scraping is made.
