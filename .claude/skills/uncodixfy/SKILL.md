# Pauli Uncodixfy Skill

## Purpose
Enforces anti-template, anti-GPT-dashboard rules for personal operations apps.

## Rules
- No decorative gradients, neon glows, glassmorphism, or card explosions.
- No floating bento grids without purpose.
- No emojis in buttons, navigation, status tags, or section headers.
- Palette: #F5F5F7 neutral bg, #FFFFFF surface, #1D1D1F text, #6E6E73 muted, #D2D2D7 border, #0071E3 blue accent.
- Typographic hierarchy over visual noise.
