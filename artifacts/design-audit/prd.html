Product Requirements Document (PRD): Chispa & Felipe

1. Objectives

Rebuild Chispa into a calm personal operations app with Apple-level design polish and integrate Felipe Kit de Arenero as an interactive, Supabase-backed project.

2. Key Specifications

  • Zero emojis across all views.
  • Navigation: Home, Projects, Shopping, Tasks, Notes, Receipts, Budgets, Inventory, Settings.
  • Felipe route: /projects/felipe-litter-kit.
  • Supabase schema: 15 chispa_* tables, strict RLS.
